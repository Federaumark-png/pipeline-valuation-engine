# Investment Memo Template

## 1. Executive Summary
Provide a concise overview of the company, the pipeline, and the key valuation outcome.

## 2. Pipeline Overview
- Company name  
- Number of assets  
- Key therapeutic areas  
- Clinical phases  

## 3. Asset-by-Asset Analysis
For each drug:
- Name  
- Indication  
- Clinical phase  
- Market size  
- Price per treatment  
- Success probability  
- Expected revenue  
- Valuation  

## 4. Total Pipeline Valuation
Summarize:
- Combined expected revenue  
- Combined valuation  
- Key drivers  
- Sensitivity factors  

## 5. Risk Assessment
Evaluate:
- Regulatory risk  
- Competition  
- Manufacturing risk  
- IP strength  
- Market adoption  

## 6. Final Recommendation
Provide a clear, professional recommendation:
- Buy / Hold / Avoid  
- Rationale  
- Key upside  
- Key downside
